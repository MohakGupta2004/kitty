# Changelog

## v1.0.0

First release.

- The cat follows the mouse cursor when it feels like it, and sits down a short
  distance away instead of on top of the pointer.
- It loses interest in a cursor that has stopped moving, and gets bored of
  chasing after a while.
- A sleep schedule: after a stretch of being awake it lies down for a nap and
  ignores the cursor completely until it wakes up.
- Petting, over-petting and being woken up rudely, each with their own reaction.
- A tray menu to toggle following, force a nap or wake the cat up, and quit.
- A JSON config file with every timing, speed and distance in it, validated at
  start-up.
- Downloadable builds for macOS (universal `Kitty.app`), Linux and Windows.
